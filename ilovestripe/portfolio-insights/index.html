01 — Discover

Admins needed clarity,
not more dashboards

We sat down directly with enterprise admins to see how they actually worked — where they got stuck, what they ignored, and what would make them trust an automated recommendation.

The Challenge

Enterprise admins faced a fragmented ecosystem with information scattered across disconnected tools. Finding and diagnosing problems took manual effort and time.

The Opportunity

By consolidating data into a single, AI-powered application, we provided enterprise admins with actionable insights and an intuitive surface to optimize and plan migrations, giving them the confidence to scale wall-to-wall with Atlassian.

02 — Define

Flying blind

Atlassian's enterprise admins managed complex, high-stakes environments — with information scattered across disconnected tools. Diagnosing problems took manual digging, and migration planning was guesswork.

Scattered data→One admin view
Manual audits→Automated insight
Guesswork→A confident migration plan

03 — Ideate

Design principles

01

Clarity over completeness

Dense, high-volume data made legible at a glance.

02

AI as a co-pilot, not a black box

An always-on assistant that summarizes, flags risk, and suggests next moves.

03

One path from insight to action

Optimization and migration planning live in the same surface.

04 — Prototype

Build it just enough to feel real

05 — Validate

Test with users. Learn, then iterate.

06 — Ship

The finished product

An application designed to help enterprise admins understand their tools and data shapes with Atlassian, optimize their footprint, and confidently prepare for cloud migration.

Data Visualizations

Crafted to make dense information easy to scan

The data visualizations turn complex, high-volume information into something admins can take in at a glance — giving them the clarity to understand their footprint and reach better decisions, faster.

AI Co-Pilot

An always-on AI co-pilot

An AI assistant sits right alongside the data — ready to summarize what's happening, flag what needs attention, and suggest the next move, so admins always have a knowledgeable second set of eyes.

Migration Planning

A clear path to cloud

Once an instance is optimized, admins move into migration planning — scoping sources, sequencing the work, and mapping a confident path to cloud, all in one place.
