Summary

The problem

Admins needed clarity, not scattered clues

Enterprise admins faced a fragmented ecosystem, with information scattered across disconnected tools. Finding and diagnosing problems took manual effort and time. They were losing their trust in Atlassian tools.

The solution

One AI-powered view, from insight to action

By consolidating data points into one intuitive surface, we were also able to extract insights and recommend actions, alongside an intelligent AI assistant to help execute. Admins could now confidently expand their footprint with Atlassian.
