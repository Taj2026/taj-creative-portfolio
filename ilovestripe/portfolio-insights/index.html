Summary

The problem

Admins needed clarity, not scattered clues

Enterprise admins faced a fragmented ecosystem, with information scattered across disconnected tools. Finding and diagnosing problems took manual effort and time. They were losing their trust in Atlassian tools.

The solution

One AI-powered view, from insight to action

By consolidating data points into one intuitive surface, we were also able to extract insights and recommend actions, alongside an intelligent AI assistant to help execute. Admins could now confidently recommend going wall to wall with our tools.

Admins worked across many different interfaces, with information scattered everywhere. They had to hunt around to diagnose a problem before they could even start fixing it, and that eroded their trust in Atlassian's tools.

The finished product

An application designed to help enterprise admins understand their tools and data shapes with Atlassian, optimize their footprint, and confidently prepare for cloud migration.

Data Visualizations

Data Visualizations

Crafted to make dense information easy to scan

The data visualizations turn complex, high-volume information into something admins can take in at a glance — giving them the clarity to understand their footprint and reach better decisions, faster.

AI Co-Pilot

AI Co-Pilot

An always-on AI co-pilot

An AI assistant sits right alongside the data — ready to summarize what's happening, flag what needs attention, and suggest the next move, so admins always have a knowledgeable second set of eyes.
