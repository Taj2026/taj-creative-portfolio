01 — Discover

Admins found out too late

This application is used by Atlassian organization administrators who manage cloud apps and sites for their companies. Any admin with an active Atlassian Cloud product and organization-level permissions can access it for free to monitor app reliability and incident history.

The Challenge

Org administrators were often not aware of Atlassian incidents or downtime until it was too late. Information was buried in disconnected dashboards and logs. Critical signals were easy to miss, leading to frustrated users in their organizations.

The Opportunity

By unifying health signals into one clear, real-time surface with constant updates, we gave teams the confidence to catch problems early and resolve them faster, together.
