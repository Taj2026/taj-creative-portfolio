Summary

The problem

Accessibility gaps blocked enterprise deals

Retrofit accessibility into a legacy platform at scale — while critical bugs blocked enterprise and public-sector deals, and no shared process existed to keep it from breaking again.

The solution

Fix it now, build it in for good

Unblock the deals immediately, and make inclusion a permanent part of how we think, design, and build at Atlassian.

Why accessibility

01 — Context

Why accessibility

Atlassian's products power governments, hospitals, banks, and universities — organizations legally and morally bound to serve everyone. If our tools couldn't be used by people relying on screen readers, keyboard navigation, or assistive tech, we weren't just failing users; we were locking Atlassian out of the enterprise and public-sector markets that demand accessibility as table stakes.

VPAT audit

02 — VPAT Audit

A VPAT audit uncovered many critical issues

By working in a deeply embedded fashion with product and engineering, our team remediated all critical issues within 9 months. This remediation consequently unblocked multiple enterprise deals, resulting in immediate revenue increase.

The Realization

04 — The Realization

Bugs weren't the problem — the system was

Fixing individual issues would only buy time. Internal and external customer research made the real pattern clear: without changing how we work, the gaps would just come back. Keeping them gone meant a complete overhaul of how we worked — and I led the design vision for it, grounded in what that research surfaced.

The Vision

06 — The Vision

From audit to embedded practice

The lasting outcome wasn't a one-time fix — it was a new way of working. We stood up a cross-functional accessibility team with shared rituals, tooling, and acceptance criteria, making accessibility a first-class input across design, build, and QA — not a late-stage audit.
